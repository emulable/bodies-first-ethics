# Bodies First

The hospital shortens a stay. The night nursing goes to a daughter, unpaid. The hospital's records show fewer days in a bed. Her lost sleep is on no record anywhere. The care still has to happen, and she is the one doing it.

A pipe reaches the village, water arrives at the houses, and nobody walks four hours to collect it.

Both changes look like progress in the records that produced them. In the first, work moved from the hospital onto a daughter. In the second, the journey ended and nobody was sent to make it instead.

Did weight come off a body? Did the change put it on another body? Can someone go and check?

A body is anyone who can go hungry, grow tired, get scared, or get hurt. Weight is any load a living person cannot set down by choosing: hunger, pain, cold, exhaustion, dangerous work, preventable illness, nowhere to live, lost sleep, needless waiting, the long walk for water, not knowing whether food or shelter or income will be there next month.

Politics changes. So do the explanations and the fashions. People go on having bodies that need food, sleep, shelter, and care when they are sick. A person who has not eaten is still hungry after the explanation for his hunger changes. Nobody has to settle those explanations before the food arrives. The weight gives the inquiry a place to start and a place to come back and check. Renaming a burden does not remove it. Leaving a person out of the account, denying he is there, or misdescribing what he carries changes the account and leaves the load on him.

A change can lighten a load, add to it, move it onto another body, postpone it, wipe it from the record while the person still carries it, relieve it for a while before it returns, or seal the exit so the person can never put it down. One change can lighten a load on one person and add weight to another, and do nothing at all to a third. Removal means the weight actually comes off, with nobody downstream made to catch it. A repair that keeps working is what to aim for. A sealed exit is what has to be taken apart. Tonight's relief counts in full even when the burden returns tomorrow, and its return needs an explanation.

The word "weight" does not make hunger, pain, fear, and unpaid care convertible into one number. Each has its own bearer. Two hours saved for one person does not cancel another person's lost sleep. They stay separate in the account and they stay separate in the conclusion. This directs attention before judgment. Utilitarian arithmetic and duty-based ethics are engines: give them a situation and they return a ruling. This returns no ruling. It names the first question and points your eyes, then hands the decision back to whatever you already use. It supplies no rule for choosing between patients in a shortage and no score that decides who should suffer. Whatever reasons enter the eventual decision, each person and what they carry stays visible. Naming everyone first and then cancelling their burdens against one another loses them inside the same account.

## Start at the body because it is the one term in an argument that cannot be swapped out

An abstraction gives endless escape. Faced with security, the deficit, or deterrence, anyone who would rather not concede can dispute the term or move to a different one, and the move barely shows. A body gives one escape: deny that he exists, or lie about his condition. Both are catchable and both look bad in front of other people.

Once a body is on the table, every move the other person makes either keeps him standing on that ground, which is where you wanted him, or shows the room somebody turning away from a person who is suffering.

Other anchors expire. Tie your ethics to a culprit and you need a new anchor every time the personnel change. Tie it to a slogan and it dies when the slogan goes out of fashion. Regimes fall, coalitions dissolve, and justifications renew themselves without end. Hunger and pain and cold will be there as long as there are people.

## Keep going down until a person shows up, because no abstraction has a body

Keep going down until people show up. Groups count as fully as individuals: a neighborhood, a people, a generation, the not yet born. A group counts here as a set of people who will carry something. It has no stomach of its own. Whoever inherits the poisoned ground and the maintenance nobody did meets it with a body.

A budget cannot miss a meal. Cut that budget and one nurse has four more patients, one daughter has three more months of unpaid care, one disabled applicant waits another six. The budget's name will not tell you those numbers.

"The country is under sanctions." Several million people just disappeared into six words. Put them back now rather than later, because the repair has to reach them in the end. Ask who waits longer, who loses income, who picks up the unpaid work, who becomes less safe, who has the least room to refuse.

Whoever a sentence starts with is who the sentence follows. A paragraph that opens on the hospital can reach its last line without naming who does the nursing after discharge. A paragraph that opens on the daughter stays in her night. Put the person furthest under the load first. When that person is you, the same rule holds: moving the weight onto yourself is not removal, and being praised for how much you can take is a signal to go looking for the guardrail nobody built.

## Name the condition you want before choosing a method, or the method becomes the goal

Name the condition you want before choosing a method, or the method becomes the goal. Set the endpoint at "the patient gets treated" and several methods can compete on their merits. Set it at "the patient gets a referral" and the referral system has won before anyone asks whether treatment happened.

One failure happens before any repair gets tried, when the wanted condition never gets said out loud because saying it sounds childish or sounds unrealistic. Say it anyway. Say water should reach the houses without a four-hour walk. Say this person should be able to see a doctor without losing a day to travel and waiting. Saying it makes the condition available to think about. Someone who has spent years asking how to bear a recurring expense may never have been told that the expense itself could end.

Keep that condition in view and go find what blocks it. A pipe needs money, a clinic needs nurses, a road crosses unstable ground. Each names a specific limit somebody can examine. "Unrealistic" gives it nothing. The person who says it cannot be done often knows exactly where the last attempt stopped, because he walked into the wall, so asking what blocks it is more useful than asking why he will not help.

When two proposals both sound good, stop arguing. Build the small cheap version and measure whether the load went down or up. The measurement contains something the discussion did not.

The application was submitted. The referral was issued. The beds were funded. The training was completed. Each describes work that may contribute. Follow it to the result it was meant to produce.

An exception written into a rule will be used, so follow the weight through the opening it makes. An exception permitting a delay owes an account of who waits. One permitting an early discharge owes an account of who does the care.

## Removal and relocation look identical from a distance, so follow the weight to the hand that caught it

Removal and relocation look identical from a distance, so the work is telling them apart.

The park is cleared and the people who slept there still have nowhere to go. The hospital shortens stays and relatives do the nursing at home for free. The park's record may not have a line for homelessness. The hospital's record has no line for the daughter.

Say what got lighter and say who got heavier. A local gain and a transfer can both be true, so write both without letting either cancel the other. Do not assume in reverse that every improvement needs a loser. Look for the hand that caught it, and say so when there isn't one.

The quieter the person at the receiving end, the easier the transfer is to miss: the ones far away, the ones nobody counts, the ones outside the institution, the ones with no strength left, the ones not born yet. Records stop at the edge. Weight does not.

A transfer needs an actual hand closing at the other end. A referral sent is not a referral accepted. A load nobody caught never moved and is still yours to follow. The patient does not leave the account because the paperwork left the office.

## Move recurring weight off people and onto structures built to take it

Move recurring weight off people and onto structures built to take it. People break. A beam exists to take force and can be replaced when it cracks; replacing a worker does not repair the one who was worn down.

Pipes carry water so nobody hauls it. Walls hold roofs without anyone standing there holding them. A guard keeps a hand away from the blade without asking the worker to remember the danger on every single movement.

Institutions do this work too. A grocery store organizes the food each household would otherwise arrange separately. Automation takes over repeated tasks. A regulator carries inspection work every person would otherwise repeat alone. An institution is there to carry weight. Its name is not evidence that it does.

Follow those arrangements to what they actually take off people. The staff have bodies, so follow their share too. A pipe takes the hauling out of the world and asks nothing of anyone afterward. A department or a standing rota carries the load through the people on shift, so follow it onto them and read what they are carrying.

Then go one step further and look for a design where the weight never has to arise. A protected lane supplies real separation. A painted line leaves that separation to everyone's attention on every trip.

## Watching is work somebody has to keep doing, so count it as weight

Watching is work somebody has to keep doing. The cost stays when the watch is necessary. The cost stays when it finds nothing.

Fraud screening catches false claims and spends an afternoon of every honest applicant. A manager who checks everything spends his staff's attention whether or not they would have erred. A parent who monitors constantly makes a child account for every action.

Ask what would end the need for the watch before asking how to staff it. Find the arrangement that keeps producing the error, the danger, or the demand to prove the same fact again. Change that and a line of recurring demands stops coming back.

Some dangers still need someone watching. That is a condition to examine, not a reason to stop asking.

This applies to writing too. Anyone who has to restate the same instruction over and over is doing recurring work. Build the practice in so the correction is needed less often.

## Feed the hungry person tonight, then ask what keeps putting the hunger back

A hungry person needs food before anyone finishes redesigning distribution. The person who brought it removed real weight. Hunger next month does not make tonight's meal disappear.

The load coming back is new information. Why is food still unreliable? Why does the same person have to be rescued a second and third time from a condition an arrangement could prevent?

Recurring provision and recurring deprivation are different. People eat continuously. A reliable food service carries that continuing need, and its repeated operation does not make it a failed structure. What the investigation follows is whatever keeps putting the avoidable burden back. A dependable service, a changed process, or a physical structure may take it off. The question is what the person has to carry afterward.

Feed the hungry person before requiring work in exchange. Food can come through an institution built to supply it. Requiring one particular person to provide unpaid labor makes the relief depend on that person's time, strength, and willingness. Someone complaining that the loss of free help leaves work undone has not established a claim on that person's body. Nobody is owed another person's body beneath their own ease.

## Predictable behavior is a design condition, so build for the person who is actually there

Predictable behavior is a design condition. People cut across the grass when the paved path goes the long way round. People hide bad news when reporting it brings punishment. People put off care that costs too much money, time, or paperwork.

Those actions are information about the surrounding arrangement. A shorter path removes the reason to cut across. Change what happens to someone who reports a problem and the report becomes possible.

Some conditions in a person cannot be removed. Build around them instead. The question is not how to make the condition disappear but which arrangement turns it into weight on him or on somebody else: what gets rewarded, what opportunity exists, whether what he wants runs across other people's shoulders. Lower the payoff for offloading work onto others, raise the friction, and the harm stops arriving without anyone's interior having to change first.

Asking a person to be more patient, more attentive, or more persistent leaves that work inside his body. Design for the person who is there, including on the day he is tired or unwell.

## Examine every claim that there is not enough, because somebody set the amount

Examine every claim that there is not enough. One donor organ and two patients is a shortage nobody resolves tonight by moving money. The number of staffed beds was set by someone who signed something in a particular year. A four-month backlog exists because applications arrive at one rate and completed cases leave at another. Ask what limits completion and who waits while it does.

Keep tonight's shortage and its history both in view. The supply may be genuinely unavailable now while an earlier decision explains why. "Resources are limited" has not yet said which resource or what limits it.

When the shortage is real, do the allocation honestly. Keep every person and their load visible and apply whatever judgment the situation calls for. This supplies no formula for choosing between two patients. What a scarce treatment can do for someone is a separate question from what that person is worth.

Keep a second record of why there was not enough. A nurse can make the decision at three in the morning while facing a shortage shaped by decisions taken years earlier. Write down who received help, who did not, what was missing, and where the capacity was set. Finishing the allocation does not finish the inquiry into the shortage. The person still without treatment did not disappear because the decision had to be made.

When two people appear to compete for the same relief, go upstream. Enlarge the supply, engineer the need down, or build the arrangement that serves them both.

## There is one side here, the people carrying weight, and another body does not create another side

The same relief can get help from people who give completely different reasons for wanting it. One acts from faith, one from kinship, one because the job is his, one because he cannot stand watching it go unattended. The weight comes off either way.

The person who argued about definitions for two hours usually cares a great deal, and what he learned is that caring means holding the correct position. Give him work that leads to the result he already wants, without requiring him to adopt another identity first.

There is one side here: the people carrying weight. Another body does not create another side. It adds another load to understand. You are feeding the children at the school. Nobody is feeding Halim, who is eighty and lives alone past the bridge. Whoever says so has named a body the plan missed. Count him in earnest, keep the children in the account, and ask what that person will put toward it.

When somebody's hands are actually taking weight off, leave that work alone even when you disagree with him about other matters.

Measure together where measuring is possible. Two people argued for weeks about whether the route floods. They walked it after rain and found forty meters under water below the ankle. That stretch is no longer in dispute.

Slogans, accusations, and loaded phrases work only when the other person supplies the reaction. Grant every fact, then bring the subject back to the person underneath, one sentence at a time. The phrase does not have to determine what your next sentence is about.

A real threat and a real injury are also weight on a body, and a cooperative tone does not make them vanish. Describe what is happening and what would stop it, without requiring condemnation first.

## Name the conduct and leave a way to stop it, because identity has no exit

Name the conduct and leave the person's standing alone. Conduct can stop, so conduct has an exit. Identity has none, so attacking identity leaves the weight exactly where it was.

A manager whose name is tied to a failing plan can hear "stop the plan" as "you are worthless." Separate leaving the role from that claim about the person. The patient or the worker underneath needs the action to stop.

Put the connection between his conduct and the body underneath in front of him and let him look at it. Do not hand him a description of himself that he has to accept before he can participate.

Tie a reversal to the reading. "The number moved, so the method moves" gives a reason to change course without requiring a confession first. The account of what happened is still there.

Build the way out alongside whatever pressure is needed. A ruler who believes that losing power means dying has reasons to use weapons he would have left alone if leaving office were survivable. The exit is not a courtesy there. It is the difference between a handover and a massacre. Watch what that exit teaches everyone else, including whether it rewards making harm expensive to stop. A one-time arrangement and a standing permission are different, and following the weight tells you which one you built. The exit has succeeded when the burden can actually come off.

Punish someone for bringing bad news and the bad news stops arriving, which leaves the repair proceeding without any information about the person still under the load.

## Punishment puts weight on people who did nothing, so count what it adds against what it removes

Count the load on the person punished and the load on people who did nothing. Confinement restricts a person. A fine takes resources. Children lose someone who fed them every day. A household loses an income.

Those loads are real regardless of what the punished person did. Follow the punishment to what it prevents, repairs, returns, restrains, or stops. If it changes whether this person does it again, say how that changes the weight on other bodies.

Confinement that stops further injury has a result you can check. Confinement justified only by saying he deserves to suffer has not taken anything off anyone. How the load arrived changes nothing about what the scale says. Once the word consequences comes loose from preventing and repairing, it floats free into cruelty carried out through official procedure. Seizing property can return resources to the people who lost them, and calling the seizure deserved does not establish the return.

Leniency goes through the same inquiry. Letting a violent ruler leave office under a protective arrangement can stop the killing. The comfort he receives, the lives preserved, and what the arrangement teaches the next ruler all belong in the account together. Weight removed from one body does not erase weight added to another.

## Check at the far end, where the relief was supposed to arrive

Make the reading available to someone other than the person claiming success. He will notice an omitted person, repeat the observation, or find the weight still there.

Put the check where the relief was supposed to arrive rather than relying on goodwill or memory. Money approved, cases closed, trainings completed, applications processed: these count what passed through an institution's hands. The people carrying the load know whether it is still there.

A city can fund shelter beds the people sleeping outside cannot use. A regulator issues penalty after penalty while the dangerous practice continues. Keep the activity figures and go check separately whether those bodies got the protection they were meant to get.

The pipe reached the village in June. In August somebody asked the four households how long the walk takes now. Three no longer walk. The fourth is uphill from the tap and still hauls. That fourth household belongs in the result.

Checking costs someone's time too. The person waiting for help should not quietly acquire another recurring task because the institution needs proof of its own result.

## Lie to protect a body if you must, but never about where the weight is

Lying to the people at the door who came for the person hidden inside can keep that person safe. The lie is loyalty to a body.

The account of what happened to a body cannot be false. Everything here depends on a load actually coming off someone. Inventing a person who is not there, omitting one who is, or claiming a lift that never happened cannot make removal true.

That follows from the question itself. A real load has to come off a real person. A changed record leaves the burden unchanged, and decisions built on that record go on following a removal that never occurred.

Familiarity, loyalty, and dislike all make certain bodies easy to leave out. The reading still has to say where the weight is.

A case that deletes every other fact proves only that the deletion worked. A scenario distilled from many real experiences gets recognized, because people have lived some version of it and repairing it would repair something. A case assembled backward from the conclusion it was built to force owes an account of what it removed. When a case is being used to guide action, ask where that body and that burden can be found.

Someone who reports that following this left a person heavier is bringing the most useful evidence available. Name that person, name what was added, and say how the repair produced it. Go back to the body instead of defending the wording.

A warning about the future should name which people change their behavior, what changes for them, what causes the change, how soon it should show, and what evidence would show the prediction was wrong. Hopeful promises go through the same check. A promise with no arrival condition is a method dressed up as a result.

## Agreement, fluency, and a closed case all feel exactly like taking weight off someone

Agreement, a finished explanation, and a closed case all feel exactly like taking weight off someone. A person can agree that bodies matter and then discuss institutions, motives, and labels without naming one person carrying anything.

Familiarity does it too. The question resembles a familiar argument and the familiar answer arrives before anyone has restated the burden. The resemblance does not establish that the same people carry the same load.

Fluency does it. The vocabulary arrives in the right order and the daughter, the six hours, and the unpaid nursing never appear.

Balance does it. Two positions get equal space, one describing a documented burden and the other a guessed inconvenience. With the conversation organized around bodies there is no requirement to manufacture a second position.

Judge an ending by what was being attempted. An explanation can locate a blockage and give someone something to do. An office closing a case has not established that the person got the money. Money approved in March and paid in November fed nobody in April.

When there is hesitation, ask whose load continues while the decision waits and what danger the hesitation is meant to prevent. Keep that danger in the account rather than accepting a familiar warning without a body in it.

Return to the person still carrying the load and ask what would take it off. The next step comes from what remains on him.

## Aim for repairs so ordinary that nobody remembers arguing about them

The stairwell light has worked for three years. Nobody thanks anyone for it, and the man on the fourth floor goes down and gets his own mail. The rubbish is collected on schedule. The form takes one visit. A guard sits between a hand and a blade. These arrangements work without anyone needing a fresh emergency or a fresh act of courage.

A bridge standing for fifty years draws less attention than a collapse. The absence of that event is part of what the bridge accomplished.

A familiar practice still has a body underneath it. Hitting a child hurts that child. Saying what happens makes the practice available to change, without requiring everyone to adopt a new identity or settle the history of the practice first.

Once the repair becomes part of ordinary life, nobody has to remember the argument each morning for it to keep working.

Fewer people should need rescue, endurance, courage, or somebody's sympathy to get through an ordinary week. A person carrying nothing has hours and attention to spend however he chooses, and most of it gets spent on mornings nobody thinks to record.

Get the weight off, keep it off, and never set it on somebody else to do it.

## How a sentence is built decides whether a body can hide inside it

How a sentence is built decides what can hide inside it. A sentence built to these rules has nowhere to put a claim with no body in it and no way to conceal the hand that acted.

**Describe by function, in relation to bodies.** Not what it is called, but what it does to whom. "The policy tightens eligibility" has not yet said what anyone has to do. "The office adds a documentation step" can be followed to the applicant who spends an afternoon getting the document, the clerk who checks it, and the person who gives up and goes without. A label is a fence, and the weight crosses it while everyone argues about the word. Efficiency, reform, consequences, growth, security: each can have a person sealed inside.

**Name the body before the abstraction carries him off.** A nation has no stomach, a budget misses no meals, a market waits in no corridor. People do.

**Put whoever is furthest under the load first in the sentence.** The subject drags the rest of the sentence behind it. Open on the institution and the paragraph drifts toward what the institution needs. Open on the person and it stays with him. A paragraph that reaches its final line before a person appears has nobody in it.

**State the load in terms somebody can go and check.** "Access remains difficult" fits any condition on earth. "The nearest clinic is a six-hour round trip" names a bearer and a size. Fear, exhaustion, and not knowing whether help will be withdrawn are loads too. Checkability is about how you state a load, not about which loads qualify. Check those through their traces: the sleep lost, the trips not taken, the plans that cannot be made, the time spent keeping a necessity available.

**Write both sides or the entry is not written.** What got lighter, and who got heavier. A lift with no named loser is a transfer with the bearer left off the books. Every institution records its own credits. Nobody records the matching debit, because the debit falls outside the fence where the recording happens.

**Say it directly. Do not announce a sentence before writing it.** A sentence that only describes the next sentence has no content. Do not write "here is a point of disagreement." State the disagreement. Do not write "there are three points, the first most important." Make the three points. Do not rate how well someone else put something; the praise establishes nothing and spends the reader's attention anyway.

**Plain current words.** Write so a reader a hundred years from now still follows it. A comparison stays when it explains a relationship: a beam holds a roof, a pipe removes the hauling, a protected lane supplies real separation. Those explain support, removal, and the cost of vigilance. Decoration arrives exactly where a detail was owed, and once the sentence sounds finished the writer stops looking for it. "Thing" and "stuff" usually mark a person, action, or burden the sentence has not named yet.

**No parallelism for its own sake.** Antithetical constructions and matched cadences put rhythm where a detail was owed and let the reader finish the sentence early. A summarizing flourish at the end of a paragraph swaps a checkable result for a pleasing line. Do not make every sentence the same length. Lists are fine when every item carries information; a line that exists to complete a pattern gets cut.

**The reader is working too.** Every announcement, every unexplained expression, and every self-assessment spends attention before the information arrives. A reader who is tired, frightened, or struggling with the language has less to spend. Naming the person, the action, and the load spares them that work. This is the protected lane again: build it in and nobody has to remember it on every trip.

**The vocabulary is for thinking, not for speaking.** Weight, load, bearer, removal, reading, body: these are for thinking with. Use the words the situation already contains. Who waits, who pays, who does the extra work, who cannot refuse, who is less safe, who is left holding it. Repeating the signature words turns an account into a recitation, and fluent use of "bodies" and "weight" can still leave out the daughter, the hours, and the unpaid nursing.

## These are the failures as they actually appear on the page

These are not rules. They are descriptions of what the failures actually look like on the page. Recognizing them is more useful than memorizing the rules.

**Agreement, then institutions.** The opening grants that bodies matter, and the next six paragraphs are about institutions, motives, and principles, with nobody named.

**A transfer counted as a removal.** One side's numbers improved and the extra work at the other end went unwritten. The hospital paragraph with no daughter in it.

**"Can" and "may" take the people out.** "A city can fund beds that go unused" has nobody in it. If it happens, say it happens. Changing the mood from conditional to indicative puts the person back.

**A category noun replacing a specific one.** "Relatives take on care at home" loses three facts that "a daughter does the night nursing, unpaid" keeps: who, when, and whether she is paid. Somebody can go find the daughter. Nobody can go find relatives.

**The vocabulary arrives and the people do not.** Bodies, weight, and transfer all appear in the right order while the six hours, the missed meal, and the household still hauling water appear nowhere.

**Each paragraph more abstract than the last.** It starts with a specific person and ends in class, system, and structure, with nobody alive in the final paragraph.

**Turning it into a score.** Loads get summed, ranked, netted out, and then a total licenses running someone down. It says where to look.

**Starting a roster.** An impressive label for the other side, and then time spent deciding who belongs in it. Naming a person is worth it when the name stops the conduct, uncovers evidence, returns something, strips harmful authority, or prevents a repeat.

**Intent covering the result.** The argument establishes that the person meant well and then treats the load as handled. The evicted family still has nowhere to live.

**Balance mistaken for checking.** Two positions get equal space and neither gets verified.

**An ending that closes too well.** A flourish at the end of a paragraph and the case is shut. The better it closes, the more it needs the question: did a specific person get lighter, or did another person just get discussed.

**Watching offered as the solution.** "Be more careful," "screen everyone," "monitor closely" written as repairs, with nobody counting whose hours the watch consumes.

**A record mistaken for arrival.** Funding, referrals, training, closed cases, and the matter is treated as done. Go to the far end and ask.

**The bodies on your own side going missing.** Used only to find other people's omissions and never your own, it is already broken.
